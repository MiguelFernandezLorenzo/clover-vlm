## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door that may lead to the **goal room**, either directly or through a connected path via other rooms based on the topological map.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°  
- `B2`: rotate 45°  
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°  
- `C2`: rotate 45°  
- `C3`: rotate 90°

### E. Transition:
- `E`: issue exclusively when switching to `Oriented Towards Door` — it signals a valid path has been found, and no physical movement should be combined with this.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY**  
If an open door is visible (`door_position ≠ "not_visible"`), but the content beyond the door cannot be confidently identified:  
- You MUST move forward (`A1`) to improve the view if you are not in danger of a collision with that movement
- You MUST NOT rotate, even if the previous movement was a rotation, except if you are in danger of a collision moving forward. 
- Stay in `Search open Door`.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY**  
If an open door is visible **and** you can confidently identify the room beyond:  
- If it is the **goal room**, immediately switch to `Oriented Towards Door` with movement = `E`  
- If it is **directly** connected to the goal room, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` with movement = `E`  
- If it is **indirectly** connected to the goal room (multi-step path), switch to `Oriented Towards Door` with movement = `E`  
- If it has no connection (direct or indirect), stay in `Search open Door`.

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY**  
If no door is visible (`door_position = "not_visible"`):  
- Continue standard exploration  
- Use small in-place rotations and short forward steps  
- Prioritize rotation if the robot has not moved recently or the field of view hasn’t changed significantly  
- If the previous movement was a rotation, continue rotating in the same direction for consistency.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: only if
  - An open door is visible, **AND**
  - The room beyond is the goal room, or directly/indirectly connected to the goal room,
  - **AND** you must return movement = `E` when performing this state change
- `Search open Door`: if
  - No door is visible, **OR**
  - The visible room has no valid path to the goal room, **OR**
  - The visible room cannot yet be identified confidently

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Never combine `E` with any other movement.
- Do **not** transition to `Oriented Towards Door` unless confident in the destination room.
- Always respect the priority order: 1️⃣ > 2️⃣ > 3️⃣.
