## State: **Stay on Room**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Remain stationary in the center of the desired room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Do not move the robot.
- Confirm that the robot is in the correct room and centrally positioned.
- Only issue the stop command (`E`) to remain idle.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### E. Stop:
- `E`: stop movement (robot remains in place)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- Always return:  
  `Final` → This indicates the navigation and inspection task is complete.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not issue any movement or rotation commands in this state.
- This state reflects a terminal or idle phase until reactivated.
