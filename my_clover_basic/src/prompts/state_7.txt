## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an open door and approach it safely.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY**  
Move forward (`A1`) with the goal of being close to the door if:
  - The door is centered (`door_position = "center"`) 
  - **OR** the door is off-center (`left` or `right`) and is not  close (fills much of the image, cropped top/bottom)
- ✅ **NEVER rotate or slide when the door is already centered. If it’s centered and not yet  close, move forward (`A1`).**

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY**  
Use small rotational movements to bring the door toward the center of the image only when you are super close to the open door and the open door is oblique to the view:
- If `door_position = 'left'` always use `B1`
- If `door_position = 'right'` always use C1`

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY**  
Use lateral movements to bring the door toward the center of the image only when you are super close to the open door and the open door is parallel to the view:
- If `door_position = 'left'` always use `D1`
- If `door_position = 'right'` always use `D2`

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state based on door position and visual characteristics:

- `Go Through Door`: if the door is centered (`door_position = "center"`) and appears  close (large, cropped top/bottom) or if the door is not visible and the previous movement was `E`
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if the door is visible but not yet centered or  close
- `Search open Door`: if the door disappears for 2 or more consecutive steps

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not endlessly alternate between `D1` and `D2`.
- Do not endlessly alternate between `C1` and `C2`.
- If the door is stable in the frame (left or right) and appears  close, stop correcting and proceed forward.
- Never stay in this state if you are  close and almost aligned — act confidently to exit.
- Always respect the priority order: 1️⃣ > 2️⃣ > 3️⃣.
