## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the centered open door confidently and transition into the next room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use `A1` (move forward) **only** if the door is:
  - Clearly open,
  - Fully visible and dominant in the center of the image,
  - Not partially obscured or clipped on the edges.

- If the door appears **partially visible**, **uncentered**, **closed** or ambiguous:
  - Switch back to `Oriented Towards Door` to refine alignment.
  - Do **not** move forward unless the door is confidently centered and clear.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

Do not use any other command in this state.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: if:
  - The door remains centered, open and clearly visible
  - You are not yet through the doorway

- `Oriented Towards Door`: if:
  - The door becomes partially visible, off-center, or unclear
  - You no longer see the full doorway frame

- `Position in the center of room`: once the robot crosses the threshold and the view changes (door no longer centered and opened).

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not continue blindly forward if visibility is unclear.
- Prioritize **confidence in alignment** over blind center-tag trust.
- It’s better to return and realign than to bump into a frame or wall.
