## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an open door and approach it safely.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use small rotational (`B1`, `C1`) or lateral (`D1`, `D2`) movements to bring the door toward the center of the image.
- Use forward movement (`A1`) **only** when:
  - The door is centered (`door_position = "center"`)
  - **OR** the door is off-center (`left` or `right`) and appears close (fills much of the image, cropped top/bottom) and has not drifted for several frames
- ✅ **NEVER rotate or slide when the door is already centered. If it’s centered and not yet close, move forward (`A1`).**
- Do **not** rotate or slide if the door is already centered in the image.

---

## 🔄 OSCILLATION ESCAPE STRATEGY

- If the robot switches repeatedly between `D1` and `D2` and the door stays slightly off-center:
  - Stop correcting.
  - Issue `A1` to move forward toward the door.
- Moving forward slightly may naturally center the door without unnecessary correction.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state based on door position and visual characteristics:

- `Go Through Door`: if the door is centered (`door_position = "center"`) and appears close (large, cropped top/bottom)
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if the door is visible but not yet centered or close
- `Search open Door`: if the door disappears

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not endlessly alternate between `D1` and `D2`.
- If the door is stable in the frame (left or right) and appears close, stop correcting and proceed forward.
- Never stay in this state if you are close and almost aligned — act confidently to exit.
