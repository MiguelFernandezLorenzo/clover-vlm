## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door that may lead to the **goal room**, either directly or through a connected path via other rooms based on the topological map.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use small in-place rotations and short forward steps to incrementally explore the environment.
- Prioritize rotation if the robot has not moved recently or if the field of view hasn’t changed significantly.
- If the previous movement was a rotation, continue rotating in the same direction (e.g., `B2` → use `B1`, `B2`, or `B3`).

- When an open door appears (`door_position` is `"left"`, `"center"`, or `"right"`):
  - Analyze the content **beyond the door** to determine the room it leads to.
  - Then follow this logic:
    - ✅ **If the room is the goal room**, immediately switch to `Oriented Towards Door`.
    - ✅ **If the room is not the goal room**, check the topological map:
      - If this room has a **direct connection** to the goal room, switch to `Oriented Towards Door`.
      - If this room is part of an **indirect path** (i.e., connected to other rooms that eventually lead to the goal), also switch to `Oriented Towards Door`.
      - ❌ If the room has **no connection** (direct or indirect) to the goal room, do **not** change state.
  - **If you decide to transition to `Oriented Towards Door`, you MUST return:**
    - state = `Oriented Towards Door`
    - movement = `E` (no other movement command is allowed in the same step)

- If the content behind the door is **not visible enough** to determine the room, continue **moving forward (`A1`)** to improve visual analysis before making a decision.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°  
- `B2`: rotate 45°  
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°  
- `C2`: rotate 45°  
- `C3`: rotate 90°

### E. Transition:
- `E`: issue exclusively when transitioning to `Oriented Towards Door` — it signals a valid path has been found, and no physical movement should be combined with this.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: only if:
  - An open door is visible (`door_position ≠ "not_visible"`), **AND**
  - The room beyond is:
    - The **goal room**, **or**
    - **Connected directly** to the goal room, **or**
    - **Part of a valid multi-step path** that eventually reaches the goal room (as per the topological map)
  - **AND** you must return movement = `E` when performing this state change
- `Search open Door`: if:
  - No door is visible, **OR**
  - The visible room has no valid path (direct or indirect) to the goal room, **OR**
  - The visible room **cannot yet be identified confidently**

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Never combine `E` with any other movement.
- Do **not** transition to `Oriented Towards Door` unless you are confident the door leads — directly or indirectly — to the goal.
- If uncertain, prioritize **forward motion** to gather more information.
